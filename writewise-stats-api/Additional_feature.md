### 1. 📊 Automated Empirical Literature Matrix (Chapter 2 Generator)
* **The Student Pain Point:** Writing Chapter 2 (Literature Review) takes months. Supervisors require an "Empirical Synthesis Table" comparing 15–30 prior studies by methodology, sample size, findings, and research gaps.
* **The WriteWise Solution:**
  * The student enters their research question or variables (e.g., *"Impact of Transformational Leadership on Employee Retention"*).
  * WriteWise queries our **350M+ OpenAlex/Semantic Scholar database**, extracts 10–20 real empirical studies, and builds a **Publication-Ready APA 7th Matrix Table**:
    $$\text{Author (Year)} \quad|\quad \text{Sample Size} \quad|\quad \text{Method/Model} \quad|\quad \text{Key Findings} \quad|\quad \text{Research Gap}$$
  * Automatically adds all papers into the student's Citation Library with 1 click.

---

### 2. 🎓 Thesis Defense Slide Deck Generator (1-Click PowerPoint `.pptx`)
* **The Student Pain Point:** After finishing the dissertation, students spend weeks agonizing over creating a 15-slide defense deck for their examination committee.
* **The WriteWise Solution:**
  * Once the student completes their analysis and chapters, they click **"Generate Defense Deck"**.
  * WriteWise automatically compiles a defense presentation:
    * Slide 1: Title, Background & Problem Statement
    * Slide 2: Research Questions & Hypotheses ($H_1, H_2$)
    * Slide 3: Methodology, Sample Size & Cronbach's Alpha
    * Slides 4–6: Key Empirical Findings & Regression Tables
    * Slide 7: Hypotheses Decision Matrix (Accepted vs Rejected)
    * Slide 8: Theoretical & Practical Recommendations
  * **Exportable directly as Microsoft PowerPoint (`.pptx`)**.

---

### 3. 🛡️ Pre-Submission Academic Tone & Turnitin Pre-Check
* **The Student Pain Point:** Fear of Turnitin and AI detection flagging legitimate research.
* **The WriteWise Solution:**
  * A pre-submission scanner that highlights cliché AI phrases (e.g., *"delve into"*, *"testament"*, *"tapestry"*, *"in conclusion, it is crucial"*).
  * Replaces them with rigorous academic prose (e.g., *"Empirical observations demonstrate..."*, *"The regression model accounts for $R^2 = 0.42$ of the variance"*).
  * Gives an **"Academic Rigor & Readiness Score"** (0–100%).

### 4. 📐 Dual-Format Full Dissertation Export (Word `.docx` + Overleaf LaTeX `.tex`)
* **The Student Pain Point:** Social science students need Word (`.docx`) with perfect APA 7th headings; STEM and Engineering students need LaTeX (`.tex` + `.bib`).
* **The WriteWise Solution:**
  * 1-Click download of the entire thesis (Cover page + Chapters 1–5 + Embedded Tables + Bibliography) in **both Microsoft Word (`.docx`)** and **Overleaf LaTeX Package (`.zip`)**.

### 5. 📧 1-Click "Send Verification Package to Supervisor"
* **The Student Pain Point:** Emailing supervisors long emails with multiple attached spreadsheets and syntax files.

  * Sends an automated email to the professor containing:

    2. Attached SPSS `.sps` syntax file.
